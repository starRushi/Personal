How to play
• Tap a Kannada word; it is spoken aloud so you train the sound.
• Then tap its English meaning. Correct = points × your combo.
• A wrong match costs a life and breaks your combo.
• Clear every pair to level up: more words, less time, and you regain a life (up to 5).
